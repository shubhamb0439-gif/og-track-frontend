Our Story

About OG Plus

OG Plus was founded with a simple idea: businesses shouldn't have to choose between affordability and quality when it comes to getting work done. What started as a small outsourcing shop serving a handful of local businesses has grown into a full-service outsourcing and automation partner trusted by companies across multiple industries.

Today, our team blends experienced offshore talent with modern automation tooling to deliver results that are faster, cheaper, and more consistent than traditional in-house hiring alone. We believe the future of operations isn't "people vs. automation" — it's people and automation, working together.

  • 2012Founded as a small back-office outsourcing provider for local businesses.
  • 2016Expanded services to include dedicated customer support and virtual assistant teams.
  • 2019Launched our automation practice, helping clients replace manual workflows with RPA and integrations.
  • 2022Opened our second delivery center to support growing client demand around the clock.
  • TodayProudly serving 150+ clients with combined outsourcing and automation solutions.
Our mission is to help businesses do more with less — without sacrificing quality.
