Who We Are

About FIKN

A federated network of member organizations united by a shared mission: knowledge for all, regardless of geography or background.

Our Mission

FIKN exists to connect grassroots knowledge networks, libraries, training centers, and volunteer educators into a single federated body — sharing resources, standardizing curricula, and amplifying local initiatives so that no community is left behind in the pursuit of learning.

Our Vision

A future in which every individual, in every region we serve, has equitable access to the knowledge, skills, and mentorship needed to build a self-sufficient livelihood and contribute meaningfully to society.

What We Do

Our Programs

Four core pillars guide every initiative undertaken by FIKN and its member networks.

01

Community Learning Centers

Establishing and supporting local learning hubs equipped with libraries, digital access points, and tutoring programs.

02

Vocational Training

Partnering with local industry to deliver skill-building courses in trades, digital literacy, and entrepreneurship.

03

Educator Fellowships

Recruiting, training, and placing volunteer educators in under-resourced schools and community centers.

04

Research & Policy

Publishing research on educational access and advocating for policy reforms that support equitable learning.
