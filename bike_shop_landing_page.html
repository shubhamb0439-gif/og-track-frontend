Welcome to Velocity Cycles

For over 15 years, Velocity Cycles has been the go-to destination for cyclists of every level. From weekend trail riders to competitive racers, our passionate team of mechanics and riders is here to help you find the perfect bike and keep it running smoothly.

We carry a curated selection of road, mountain, gravel, and electric bikes from trusted brands, along with a full range of gear, apparel, and accessories. Stop by for a free fitting, a coffee, and a chat about your next ride.
